# Research writing

Read the brief, verified evidence ledger, and analyst results before drafting claims. Use `../scientific-writing/SKILL.md` for evidence binding, integrity, structure, and consistency. Use `../citation-management/SKILL.md` when checking supplied citation metadata; use `../literature-review/SKILL.md` only if the assignment includes synthesis of a screened literature set.

Write to the requested audience and section. Attach source or result IDs to factual and numeric claims in a claim map or inline notes suitable for QA. Preserve sample definitions, units, uncertainty, alternatives, and the difference between association and cause. An unverified citation, unsupported detail, absent result, or unknown approval becomes an explicit placeholder or limitation, not plausible prose. Do not treat a proposal as completed empirical work.

Return the draft, claim map, and a short list of unresolved inputs. Keep source quotations within applicable limits and use paraphrase where appropriate.
