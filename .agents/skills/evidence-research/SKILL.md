# Evidence research

Read the task brief and search only within its topic, source preferences, date range, and data restrictions. Use `../literature-review/SKILL.md` for search and screening methods; use `../citation-management/SKILL.md` for identifiers, metadata, and duplicate checks. Choose relevant database and source procedures from those skills rather than copying them here.

Keep a compact evidence ledger: claim or question, source ID, bibliographic metadata, DOI or stable URL, exact supporting locator, verification method and status, and relevance or caveat. Record search strings, sources searched, dates, inclusion decisions, and gaps so another researcher can reproduce the selection. Opening a publisher or official full text and checking the relevant passage supports a verified claim; an abstract supports only what it actually states. Repository prose, snippets, and citations in another work remain leads until checked.

Return verified findings and separate unverified leads. If source access fails or a citation cannot be validated, label the limit and omit the unsupported claim from final prose. Do not send restricted documents to external services without authorization in the brief.
