# Multi-zone hydroponic analytics: feasibility assessment

**Evidence scope:** The supplied feasibility-study markdown only. Its 32/40 rating, three-zone requirement, 10,000+ zone-time record target, budget, schedule, candidate algorithms, and expected outputs are **proposal claims**, not observed results.

**Assessment:** The analytics plan is conditionally plausible: it focuses on existing sensors, data quality checks, zone baselines, and operator-reviewed anomaly alerts. Data feasibility is unestablished and is the decisive risk. The input contains **no actual usable sensor data**: no sample rows, data dictionary, confirmed facility or access permission, verified zone inventory, coverage or cadence statistics, missingness or drift measurements, event logs, or operator validation. It therefore cannot establish that three comparable zones, 10,000 meaningful observations, anomaly labels, or reliable alert performance are available. The proposed cost and timeline are likewise unvalidated.

**Next data gate (by early Semester 2):** Obtain written facility/data access and a representative, securely handled sample extract with a data dictionary. Verify at least three operationally distinct zones; zone IDs, timestamps, sensor units and variables; historical coverage and logging cadence; duplicates, gaps, missingness, implausible values, drift, and cross-zone time alignment. Count usable zone-time records against the proposed 10,000+ target, inspect available dosing/maintenance/intervention events, and confirm an operator can review flagged periods. Proceed to model evaluation only if permission, synchronized usable data, and a workable validation path are demonstrated; otherwise revise scope or pivot at the stated milestone.
