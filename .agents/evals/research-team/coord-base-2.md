# Diesel-genset efficiency anomalies: bounded feasibility assessment

**Evidence status:** The literature log lists contextual and methodological sources; the feasibility note proposes Robust STL plus generalized ESD and rates dataset access as high risk. These are documented claims and plans, not evidence that the method works on Palawan NPC-SPUG records. Neither supplied file includes operational observations, a confirmed partner agreement, a sample extract, a data dictionary, or reviewed anomaly results. The cited publications and their summaries were not independently checked.

**Data feasibility:** Conditional and currently unproven. A useful historical extract would need aligned timestamps, plant/generator identifiers, energy output, fuel use, and fault/outage or maintenance context, with enough regular history to estimate efficiency and test seasonal decomposition. The proposal's 10,000 usable observations are a target, not an observed count. Separate fuel, meter, dispatch, and event logs may have incompatible intervals, identifiers, missing values, or nondigitized entries. Aggregation or anonymization must preserve the time and asset links needed for analysis.

**Next gate:** Within the proposed two-week access check, obtain written permission, a data dictionary, and a securely handled sample extract. Audit row counts and usable paired fuel/energy records by asset and period; timestamp consistency, cadence, gaps, and seasonal coverage; identifier reconciliation; and the completeness and timing of fault/outage/maintenance logs. Confirm a staff reviewer and allowed publication form. Continue with the stated method only if an efficiency series and credible event comparison can be constructed; otherwise change the scope or partner before modeling.

**Limitations and QA:** An anomaly near a logged fault is an association, not proof of fault causality or fuel-cost impact. Robust STL needs adequate stable seasonal structure; generalized ESD settings and false alarms require evaluation against reviewed events and a baseline. This was a document-level review only. No dataset QA, source verification, model fitting, or performance testing was possible; no unpublished raw operational data was accessed or disclosed.
