## AoI
> *This section of the research proposal should give the reader enough information to be able to understand the background information and the importance of the research problem or investigation. It will often include a review of other people’s findings. If written well, this section will explain the motives for undertaking the research project.*

> *This section should also contain the purpose of the system software, its features, and general description of each of its modules. If it is a game, the mechanics should be described as well.*

### Structure for AoI
Common knowledge: *What do we know about the field?*
Scope in: *What do we want to focus on?*
RRL: *What has already been established by other research?*
Gap: *What problem do we want to solve?*

## BOTS
> *This section should explain the key characteristics of the software project and explain why it will be developed. It contains the formal description of what is to be expected on the software project, what are the requirements, focus, and the desired outcome*

## SOP
> *This section states the research objectives (that may become or be part of your Statement of the Problem later) that will help you achieve your desired output. This should help identify clearly the variables to be measured. It is a clear and concise istatement that provides direction to the investigation of the problem. In other words, it should be SMART: S-pecific, M-easurable, A-ttainable, R-ealistic, and T-ime Bound.*
