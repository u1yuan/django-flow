# Hydroponic feasibility draft (baseline 2)

The proposed multi-zone hydroponic analytics study is feasible **only if** a partner grants written access to an existing facility with at least three operationally distinct zones and supplies enough usable, time-aligned sensor and operational records for zone comparisons. The feasibility study proposes 10,000 or more meaningful zone-time observations as a target, plus preprocessing for missing readings, drift, and sensor faults; that count and the usefulness of the records remain unconfirmed. A prioritized-alert workflow also depends on an operator who can review events, while the planned schedule calls for partner and data validation by early Semester 2. Without verified access, data quality, and operator participation, feasibility remains a conditional proposal rather than a demonstrated result.

## Unresolved inputs

- Partner identity, written permission, zone definitions, and data ownership terms.
- Available variables, timestamps, logging frequency, historical span, missingness, drift, and event/maintenance logs.
- Number of usable zone-time observations after quality checks, plus operator availability for alert review.
