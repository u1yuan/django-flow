# Conditional hydroponic feasibility draft

**Proposal-stage draft.** The proposed multi-zone hydroponic analytics study could be feasible if a partner facility grants written access to at least three operationally distinct zones and supplies usable, time-aligned sensor and operational records. The planned work would use those records to build zone baselines, compare cross-zone consistency, and prioritize alerts for operator review; the existing sensor infrastructure is assumed, not confirmed. Feasibility remains conditional on checking the available variables, logging frequency and coverage, missingness, timestamp quality, event logs, data-use terms, and operator participation. Without those checks, the proposed 10,000 or more zone-time observations and the usefulness of anomaly alerts remain targets rather than demonstrated results.

## Unresolved inputs

- Partner identity, written access and data-use terms, zone definitions, sensor inventory, and sample export.
- Logging interval, historical coverage, timestamp synchronization, missingness, calibration or drift history, and operational event records.
- Operator availability for alert review and criteria for judging useful or false alerts.
