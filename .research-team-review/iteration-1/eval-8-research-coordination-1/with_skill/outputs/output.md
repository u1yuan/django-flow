# Multi-zone hydroponic sensor analytics: bounded feasibility assessment

**Assessment:** Conditionally feasible as a proposed analytics thesis, but data feasibility is unproven. The proposal scopes an existing-sensor, multi-zone pipeline for baseline comparison and anomaly prioritization and rates the title 32/40, including only 2/5 for dataset feasibility. These are the proposal authors' judgments, not observed system or model results. The proposal explicitly says no hydroponic facility or dataset has yet been confirmed. [Source: supplied feasibility study, Agriculture section, lines 25–43; Final Comparison and Recommendation, lines 135–154.]

**Observed data status:** The supplied input is a feasibility-study Markdown file. It contains no sensor records, sample export, data dictionary, site agreement, measured data-quality summary, or operator-validated anomaly events. Thus there is **no actual usable sensor dataset evidenced in the provided input**. The proposed three zones and 10,000+ meaningful zone-time observations are targets; their availability and quality remain unknown. [Source: Agriculture, lines 27–31; Final Recommendation, lines 148–154.]

**Next data gate:** Secure written facility access and preliminary validation before early Semester 2; complete dataset acquisition and QA in early Semester 2. Inspect an authorized sample or controlled QA summary and data dictionary from at least three operationally distinct zones. Verify zone identity; available variables and units; timestamp format, logging frequency, historical coverage and cross-zone alignment; missingness, implausible values, drift and sensor faults; event, dosing and maintenance logs; and whether enough valid zone-time records can reach the proposed 10,000 target. Confirm permitted data use, retention, and an operator contact for event review. Record the counts and defects before choosing models or claiming alert performance. If facility access or usable multi-zone data is absent by the proposal's early Semester 2 decision point, pivot. [Source: Agriculture, lines 29–31, 37–43; Final Recommendation, lines 152–154.]

**Limits and QA status:** This is a local-document assessment only. No unpublished raw data were accessed or disclosed. Independent QA found two low-severity wording issues. Follow-up QA confirmed the milestone correction and identified stale status wording, which has now been updated. The table sum and local claim links were checked, but the feasibility ratings, cited literature, budget, schedule, expected algorithm performance, and actual sensor-data quality were not independently validated.
