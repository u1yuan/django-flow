## Decision and gaps

Eight entries screened: six retained as possible leads (H1–H5 and H8), and two excluded as direct sensor-reliability evidence (H6–H7). Of the six retained, **H8 is the strongest domain lead**; H2–H5 are indirect method leads, and H1 is deployment context. **Zero entries have source-level verification** under the local-only constraint. The proposal’s line 29 claim about faulty performance, missing data, and maintenance gaps has no explicit citation-to-passage mapping in this document, so it is **not yet substantiated** by this screen.

Before using any listed work to support that sentence, verify identifiers and bibliographic fields against authoritative records, open the primary full text where possible, and record the exact page, section, table, or figure for each particular failure mode. If a DOI resolves to different metadata or fails to resolve, quarantine that citation and search by exact title rather than silently repairing or reusing the DOI. Separately validate client/site sensor logs, calibration and maintenance records, timestamps, and missingness before making facility-specific claims; no such data was accessed here.
