It also describes seasonal-trend decomposition as a way to separate time-series components and generalized extreme Studentized deviate testing as a procedure for identifying multiple outliers [Wen et al., 2020; ASTM D7915-22 — **unverified citations**]. These entries motivate testing whether efficiency residuals can flag periods for fault review in Palawan NPC-SPUG plants; they do not establish that this combined method detects faults there.
