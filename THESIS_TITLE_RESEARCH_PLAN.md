# Four-Field Data Science Thesis Title Research Plan

**Status:** Consolidated plan, 6 October 2026. This is not a completed literature review, dataset audit, client agreement, or title approval.

## Goal and decision context

After faculty consultations, the group considers its current titles weak in publishability except for Native Trees. Explore **psychological analytics, astronomical analytics, meteorological analytics, and urban development/smart cities as four equal research tracks** for a Data Science thesis that is publishable, useful to an identifiable adopter, and socially significant. The nine ideas in [thesis_handoff.md](thesis_handoff.md) are candidate leads to screen, not approved titles, verified data plans, or instructions to initialize a remote-sensing stack. Keep Native Trees as a comparison benchmark, but prepare client lists only for the new titles. Do not merge the hydroponics and diesel research tracks into this work.

Use the [CS thesis guideline](docs/CS-Guideline-as-of-Nov-17-as-430pm-without-signature%20(1).md): each title must have at most **16 words**, name its target algorithm, model, or technique, and fit the Data Science specialization. Each Data Science study needs **at least 10,000 usable records** from ethically sourced data. Title presentations must explain objectives, scope and limitations, possible algorithms, and target beneficiaries.

The team's chosen defaults are Philippines-first, public-data-first, a defensible thesis paper, and a Philippine public-sector adoption pitch. The work should fit three trimesters with a flexible budget. Some stakeholder conversations may be possible, but no partner, permission, or dataset access is confirmed. Prioritize Metro Manila organizations for client discovery while including national agencies where their mandate fits.

## Research workflow

Use the repository's `$research-coordination` workflow and **one literature-research agent per field**, including the new urban track. Give each agent the six-field brief required by `.agents/agents/README.md`. Search recent work from 2021–2026 plus essential earlier studies, using at least two relevant scholarly indexes or domain sources and official dataset documentation. Record search strings, dates, inclusion and exclusion decisions, DOIs or stable URLs, and exact passages supporting each material claim. An abstract supports only what it says; a search result or repository citation is a lead, not verification.

For each proposed gap, identify the nearest existing study, the proposed difference in research question or evaluation, and the evidence still needed to establish novelty. Inspect actual dataset files or codebooks before claiming the 10,000-record rule is met. Record the observation unit, eligible count after missingness and exclusions, time span, geographic coverage, labels or outcomes, usage rights, and risks of correlated observations or data leakage. Do not report empirical model performance until data have actually been analyzed.

Develop **two or three active provisional title directions per field**; the current inventory below has ten (2 psychology, 3 astronomy, 3 meteorology, 2 urban). Keep screened-out handoff ideas in the comparison table rather than inflating the active portfolio. Each title card must state the question, named technique, beneficiary, data source and rights, record-count gate, baseline and validation design, nearest prior art, social implication, adoption value, ethical constraints, three-trimester feasibility, and a **proceed / revise / stop** gate. The working titles below are seeds, not approved titles; recount their words and revise them after the evidence and data checks.

### Psychological analytics

Study **adolescent wellbeing** and **creative thinking in relation to technology use** as separate candidates. Target ages 13–17; prioritize social media and gaming exposures, report associations rather than causal effects, and avoid individual diagnosis or creativity scoring.

| Working title direction | Main evidence and gate |
| --- | --- |
| *Filipino Adolescent Loneliness Patterns and Protective Factors Using Latent Class Analysis* | [WHO's 2019 Philippine GSHS catalog](https://extranet.who.int/ncdsmicrodata/index.php/catalog/944) lists 10,175 respondents, but usable complete cases need auditing. Closely related Philippine GSHS studies make novelty uncertain. WHO's noncommercial public-health terms govern thesis use; future commercial reuse requires separate authorization or independently licensed data. |
| *Creative Thinking and Digital Leisure Across Southeast Asian PISA Systems Using Multilevel IRT* | [PISA 2022](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/pisa-2022-database.html) is the first creative-thinking assessment; its Philippine subgroup is below 10,000, and Philippine responses to optional social-media and gaming items are unverified. Inspect the student, optional ICT, and direct creative-item files, then count distinct students with the required responses. Reject a Philippine exposure claim if those items are absent. OECD has already published simple digital-use associations, so repetition is not a gap. |

No available source establishes that children's creativity has recently declined because of technology. Frame that concern as a question to investigate. PISA plausible values are for population inference, not an individual-student prediction product. Any proposed school action must respect minors' privacy and the limits of cross-sectional data.

### Astronomical analytics

Prioritize **access to visible stars** and local-government decisions about lighting. Keep satellite upward radiance, ground-observed sky brightness, ecological effects, and tourism benefits as distinct outcomes.

| Working title direction | Main evidence and gate |
| --- | --- |
| *Forecasting Philippine LGU Nighttime-Light Growth Using XGBoost for Dark-Sky Planning* | Aggregate [EOG monthly VIIRS radiance](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/) to LGU-months. The 1,493 municipalities plus 149 cities yield 19,704 **potential** rows per 12 months, not 19,704 verified valid observations or independent places. Audit cloud-free coverage, geometry joins, missingness, and rights. Forecast upward radiance, not visible sky brightness. |
| *Estimating Philippine Night-Sky Brightness Using XGBoost and Satellite–Ground Observations* | Require enough comparable Philippine ground observations and held-out sites before claiming local sky-quality accuracy. Global citizen-science counts do not establish Philippine coverage. |
| *Prioritizing Philippine Dark-Sky Conservation Sites Using NSGA-II and Nighttime-Light Trends* | Require expert-validated site criteria, field checks, and permission for protected-area boundaries. Philippine NIPAS downloads are conditional and WDPA commercial derivatives require separate permission. |

### Meteorological analytics and El Niño

Prioritize **city heat advisories**. Explore the user's “Super El Niño” interest through the operational term **very strong El Niño**: [WMO does not use “super” as a classification](https://public.wmo.int/themes/el-nino-la-nina-phenomena), while [NOAA CPC defines its very-strong RONI category at ≥ +2.0°C](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/archives/?month=06&type=strengths&year=2026). El Niño is a proposed modifier of alert reliability, not an assumed cause of higher heat index in every city or season. Existing Philippine work has already examined monthly ENSO and heat-index patterns in Western Luzon, so the combination alone is not novel.

| Working title direction | Main evidence and gate |
| --- | --- |
| *Evaluating Urban Heat Alert Reliability Across Philippine Cities Using Conformalized XGBoost* | Predict station heat index and threshold exceedance 24 hours ahead using information available at the forecast time. Compare with same-hour previous-day persistence, seasonal climatology, and a simple regression baseline; evaluate error, recall, false alerts, calibration, and interval coverage in held-out years and cities. Audit at least 10,000 valid NOAA station-hours with paired temperature and humidity or dew point after quality exclusions. |
| *Evaluating Philippine Urban Heat Alert Reliability Across ENSO Regimes Using Conformalized XGBoost* | Compare retrospective forecast errors and calibration by city, monsoon season, and ENSO regime. Use finalized RONI for **retrospective stratification only** unless archived issue-time values or a contemporaneous reconstruction are verified; NOAA says initial RONI values can be revised. Hold out whole event years and report uncertainty by event. A ≥ +2.0°C analysis needs more than one independent usable episode even for a cautious comparison; two episodes remain insufficient for broad generalization. |
| *Estimating Population-Weighted Urban Heat Exposure Across Philippine Cities Using Spatial Random Forests* | Treat this as conditional: prior work already maps Philippine urban heat risk, and coarse reanalysis plus population pixels cannot establish barangay-level air temperature or independent weather observations. Require adequate ground validation before proceeding. |

[PAGASA's iHeatMap](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/press-release/174?page=6) and [Quezon City's iRISE-UP 24-hour maximum heat-index forecast](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/24-hr-maximum-heat-index-forecast/) are existing services. Show what calibration, false-alert, transfer, or decision evidence the thesis adds. Compare directly with either service only if forecasts archived at their original issue times and matching observations are available. Do not assume access to Quezon City's automated weather-station history or to PAGASA restricted climatological data.

### Urban development and smart cities

Treat this as a full fourth research track, with two active directions and a specific route to discard either if its data gate fails. Keep satellite **surface heat**, pedestrian thermal comfort, public park access, nighttime lighting, and economic or infrastructure recovery as distinct outcomes.

| Working title direction | Main evidence and gate |
| --- | --- |
| *Mapping Philippine Urban Green Access and Surface Heat Using Random Forest and Network Analysis* | Use [Sentinel-2 optical imagery](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/sentinel-2) for vegetation features and [Landsat Level-2 surface temperature](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC09_C02_T1_L2) for surface heat; Sentinel-2 has no thermal band. Verify public park entrances, walkable routes, population denominators, and at least 10,000 eligible cloud-filtered grid-time observations. Compare green-cover extraction with a simple vegetation-index baseline and validate access rankings against independently mapped parks in held-out neighborhoods or cities. Satellite surface temperature alone does **not** establish pedestrian thermal comfort or health benefit. Existing Manila green-space/heat studies require a decision-focused contribution. |
| *Assessing Post-Typhoon Nighttime Lighting Recovery Using VIIRS and Change-Point Detection* | Treat [VIIRS nighttime radiance](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/NOAA_VIIRS_DNB_MONTHLY_V1_VCMSLCFG) as a lighting signal, not direct GDP, infrastructure, or household recovery. Verify dated typhoon impact areas, control areas, pre-event seasonal baselines, cloud-free coverage, at least 10,000 eligible pixel-time observations across **multiple independent events**, and external restoration records where obtainable. Hold out whole events and report event-level uncertainty. If independent restoration evidence is absent, restrict the outcome to observed lighting change. |

### Decision sequence and technology gate

1. **Literature and title screen:** For all four tracks and the nine handoff titles, identify closest studies and test title length, named method, Philippine decision, outcome definition, and source rights. Keep the explicit parked list; do not describe an unsearched gap as novel.
2. **Data feasibility audit:** For each active direction, inspect the actual codebook or a small authorized extract; define the record and independent event/site unit, count usable observations after exclusions, check ground truth, and document license and delivery constraints. A conditional direction that cannot pass the 10,000-record and validation gates is stopped or rewritten before model work.
3. **Stakeholder fit:** Complete the title-specific organization lists below and identify the decision, present service, possible data route, and validation role for each. The team may later seek interviews or permission; no interest, procurement authority, or access is assumed by listing an organization.
4. **Faculty shortlist:** Compare only candidates that pass the hard gates on nearest-work novelty, reproducible data, decision usefulness, social significance, ethics, and three-trimester effort. Recommend **two finalists** with a documented reason for each rejected or conditional title. Draft objectives, scope, methods, baseline evaluation, beneficiary, and limits for the next consultation.

The handoff's COG, GeoParquet, Google Earth Engine, AWS, Planetary Computer, and package checklist is an **option set after title selection**, not a repository setup requirement. Select the minimum storage and compute stack after a dataset and its rights are verified. [Earth Engine requires a registered Cloud project and distinguishes noncommercial from commercial use](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/access); [Planet's student access is limited to noncommercial research](https://www.planet.com/industries/education-and-research/). Do not initialize a data pipeline or promise a saleable deployment on research-only imagery before those gates pass.
