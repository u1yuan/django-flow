# Caliber screen

Compiled: 6 October 2026. The group asked for the same caliber as a study that estimates the association between biodiversity and nighttime light. The opened example is the STATYX Curiosity Cup 2026 manuscript in `evidence-ledger.md`. It is a competition paper about Metro Manila birds, not a journal article, and its threshold is not reused as a fact.

A topic is at that caliber only if all of these are true:

1. There is a measured exposure and a separate measured outcome.
2. The question is their association, after named confounders. It is not a claim that one caused the other.
3. The record unit is a place-time, such as a grid cell-month, not a raw catalog row and not a scraped sentence.
4. The title names the technique and stays within 16 words.
5. A path to 10,000 records of that unit is stated, and any unopened count stays unopened.
6. Someone could use the association (a city environment office, a conservation group, PAGASA) without being told a person, firm, or species was at fault.

## What falls below the bar

These ideas stay in the pools. They are not the shortlist.

| ID | Why it is a different kind of study |
| --- | --- |
| O01, P05, FiReCS | Sentiment labels on product reviews. No exposure field and no ecological or health outcome. |
| O03 | A game recommender. |
| C02 | A description of one old transit schedule. Stop-times clear 10,000 lines. That does not make an association. |
| L01 | A red-flag score on a contract. A flag is not an association between two measured conditions, and it is not evidence of wrongdoing. |
| S01 | A classifier for class suspensions. HabagatPlus already fit Naive Bayes. The public training file was not opened. |
| A02 | A satellite pass calculator, unless a sighting log is the outcome. No sighting log was opened. |
| P04 | Bullying text, with a real chance the writers are minors. |

## What can sit at the bar

| ID | Exposure | Outcome | Unit | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Nighttime radiance | Bird species richness outside Metro Manila, with an effort control | grid cell-month | Conditional. The Metro Manila version is already in the STATYX manuscript. |
| B2 | Nighttime radiance | Insect or bat occurrence | grid cell-month | Conditional. The manuscript names this as future work. That taxon's count was not opened. |
| B3 | Nighttime radiance or night heat around the respondent | Adult life satisfaction | adult respondent, then joined to a place | Stop for a Philippine-only file. Wave 7 Philippines is 1,200 cases. |
| B4 | Taglish review text | Star or sentiment class | review | Below the bar. Listed so the contrast is explicit. |
| B5 | Nighttime radiance | Nighttime land surface temperature | grid cell-month | Conditional. Cell-months were not counted. Baldres, Principe, and Soriano (2023) already report a provincial correlation between VIIRS and PSA urbanization. This is not that correlation. |
| B6 | Gridded rainfall | Provincial retail rice price | province-month | Conditional. The opened PSA old series ends in January 2021, and a province-month price file was not counted. |

B1 and B2 are astronomy only in the sense that the exposure is artificial light. The outcome is biological. B5 is meteorology. B3 is psychology and does not clear the record gate. B6 is the trade idea rebuilt as an association. No domain in the briefs below is asked to carry a sentiment or recommender study.
