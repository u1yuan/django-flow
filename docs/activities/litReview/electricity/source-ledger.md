# Electricity thesis source ledger and fit screen

Checked 2026-10-28. Scope: the three electricity topics in the approved plan only. This is a source and schema screen, not a row-count result. Publisher-reported totals are not eligible prediction-target counts. The accompanying data audit must record a dataset version, local file hash, cleaning rules, and actual target counts before any 10,000-record gate is marked passed.

## 2020-onward cutoff update

The user requires all observations to be dated 2020-01-01 or later. Use [RECON-SL](https://doi.org/10.21227/n1dk-q860) for household alerts (collection 2022–2025), [COFACTOR Drammen v3](https://zenodo.org/records/14752397) as an institutional candidate after filtering to 2020+ (source spans 2018–2021/2022), and [iFlex](https://zenodo.org/records/8248802) for a dynamic-price household candidate (winter 2020–2021). [BDG2](https://github.com/buds-lab/building-data-genome-project-2) and [London dToU](https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/smartmeter-energy-consumption-data-in-london-households-vqm0d) are excluded as observation datasets because their available recordings or tariff cohort predate 2020. Post-2020 source files, coverage and eligible counts still require inspection. Rows L1 and B1 below preserve the initial-source verification record only; they are **not active candidates** under the current date rule.

For local Philippine context, [NGCP's operations page](https://www.ngcp.ph/operations) lists hourly grid demand in MW; a [DOE FOI response](https://www.foi.gov.ph/requests/dataset-for-electricity-daily-consumption-from-jan-1-2010-to-dec-31-2020/) links its [hourly-demand workbook](https://www.ngcp.ph/Content/uploads/document/operations/Hourly%20Demand%20per%20Grid.xlsx). Keep 2020+ rows only. This grid-level data cannot validate customer or building meter forecasts. DOE's [energy statistics page](https://doe.gov.ph/data-and-prices/energy-statistics) contains aggregate statistics. No public Philippine household or institutional interval data source matching the locked targets was confirmed; local testing requires an authorized 2020+ export.

### Exclusions and caveats to carry into the audit

- Exclude RECON-SL monthly records from the 10,000 **daily target** count. The 15-minute count is only a source-file count. Preserve cumulative readings; mark negative differences, suspected resets, duplicates, and incomplete days explicitly.
- Exclude London's flat-rate customers from the dynamic time-of-use cohort. Do not multiply a 2013 dToU price by an unverified household or interval; do not infer Philippine tariff performance from London.
- Exclude BDG2 heating, cooling, steam, water, solar, gas and irrigation meters from the institutional electricity target. The paper's roughly 53.6 million measurements across all types are not an electricity meter-hour count.
- No opened source establishes eligible target counts after cleaning or local predictive accuracy. The 10,000 gate remains **open** for all three topics.
