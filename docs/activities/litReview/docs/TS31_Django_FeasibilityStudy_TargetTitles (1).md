| **Project Overview**  |
| Selecting suitable Philippine native tree species for reforestation requires evaluating soil properties, microclimate, elevation, slope, growth characteristics, disease resistance, species compatibility, and environmental requirements. Although experts and guidebooks are available and can provide reliable guidance, assessing numerous planting zones can require substantial time, while general environmental data may not capture local variations. This project proposes an explainable GIS- and edge-assisted decision support system that combines GIS data with locally collected soil and microclimate measurements and expert-validated species requirements. The system will generate ranked species recommendations, suitability scores and classifications, explanations, warnings, field-monitoring records, and GIS-based suitability maps for each assessed site or zone.
