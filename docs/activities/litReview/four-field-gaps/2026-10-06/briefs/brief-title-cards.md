# Brief — competition-level title cards

Research question or topic:
Turn each candidate gap in the assigned field file into a title card a judging panel could read, without adding evidence the gap file does not contain.

Requested output:
`docs/activities/litReview/four-field-gaps/2026-10-06/title-cards-<field>.md`

Input locations:
- The assigned `gap-candidates-<field>.md` in this same folder. That file is the only evidence.
- This brief.
- Skills: `.agents/skills/research-writing/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/scientific-writing/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/citation-management/SKILL.md`.

Constraints:
- One card per gap card, in the same order. Do not drop a card and do not add a gap.
- Do not open new literature, fit a model, or contact anyone.
- Do not upgrade "indexing not confirmed", "abstract only", "blocked", or "Philippine rows not confirmed" into a verified claim.
- Associations stay associations. No individual diagnosis of a minor. No legal or health label from imagery alone.
- Impact metrics are targets to test later, not results.
- Title: at most 16 words, form "[action] [outcome] for [context or user] Using [named technique]". Show the word count. You may keep the draft title when it already meets this form.
- Pitch: about 30 words on the problem and the solution.
- Ethics line: the main risk to the affected population and how the design limits it.
- Hydroponics and diesel stay out. Do not commit. Write only your output file.

Acceptance criteria:
Each card has these headings: Named user and decision; Demonstrable artifact; One-sentence innovation; Measurable impact against a baseline; Transfer or scale; Title; Pitch; Ethics line; Claim map. The innovation sentence must point to a specific nearest study and limitation already written in the gap card. The claim map lists each factual sentence and the gap-card section it comes from. Unresolved inputs are listed at the end of the file.

Data handling restrictions:
No datasets are downloaded. No raw survey or imagery files are read.
