## Thematic synthesis

**Surface heat is already mapped for Metro Manila.** The opened 2022 abstract uses Landsat 7 NDVI, NDBI, and land surface temperature (D8). The opened 2019 PDF uses Sentinel-3 fractions and land surface temperature (D10). The opened 2024 PDF correlates MODIS land surface temperature with PAGASA station heat index at 1 km (D9). The 2024 and 2025 abstracts add a MODIS grid for Manila with greenery and a 1 km greenspace-accessibility association (D11, D12). A thesis that only repeats a citywide vegetation-versus-surface-temperature map would retrace opened work. Whether random forest plus network distances to park entrances is unoccupied was not established. The Semantic Scholar queries aimed at park access returned HTTP 429.

**The sensors do not measure the comfort or access outcomes.** Sentinel-2 Level-2A, as opened, supports vegetation, soil, and water cover (D1, D2). Landsat 9 Level-2 `ST_B10` is surface temperature in kelvin (D3). Broqueza et al. show that MODIS land surface temperature can correlate with station heat index (D9). That correlation does not make a Landsat pixel a pedestrian comfort or health measurement. Almadrones-Reyes and Dagamac’s abstract attributes discomfort to Metro Manila warming without naming a comfort instrument (D8). Delina, Pagkalinawan, and Macagba’s abstract says 1 km land-surface-temperature products missed microhotspots that participants identified (D11). Vegetation pixels and scene-classification vegetation are not public entrances.

**Nighttime radiance is a lighting series.** The opened VIIRS page defines monthly average radiance and tells users to check cloud-free coverage, especially in the tropics (D5). Opened disaster abstracts derive recovery metrics from that kind of series (D13, D14, D15). Li et al. go on to call the result economic recovery (D14). Pagkalinawan et al. use nighttime light “to approximate areas of economic activities” (D12). Those are author labels. They are not a reason to equate `avg_rad` with GDP, restored infrastructure, or household recovery. No Philippine typhoon nighttime-light paper was retained from the title-screened rows. That is a limit of the screened rows, including a failed Semantic Scholar retry, not a demonstration that no such paper exists.

**Change-point detection was not confirmed as the standard method in the opened set.** Lin et al. name Automated Valley Detection (D13). Mansour et al. name GWR and MGWR (D15). Li et al. name regression after seasonal decomposition (D14). The Semantic Scholar query on change-point detection and nighttime lights was not completed because of HTTP 429. The arXiv check returned two unopened titles and no opened change-point paper.

**Mobility cannot carry the handoff economic-recovery title.** New Community Mobility Reports stopped on 15 October 2022 (D7). A current Philippine mobility series was not opened.

**High-resolution housing labels are not available on the opened Planet terms.** Education and Research imagery is limited, non-commercial, and not for products that might be sold (D6). This screen did not open a Philippine tenure statute. The informal-settlement title is stopped because appearance on imagery is not a tenure label, and because the opened Planet terms do not supply a commercial or unrestricted label archive.
